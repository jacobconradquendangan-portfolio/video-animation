## PART 2: Keyframe Motion Choreography & Animation Blueprint

1. **The Paper Vulnerability Shift (Act I):** 
   - Parallax splitting of the top-down macro shot into 3 2.5D depth layers. 
   - Ink bleeding/scattering on the word *"fragile"*, followed by a lightning-fast vector tear-away wipe from left to right.
2. **The Multi-Module Ecosystem Expansion (Act II):** 
   - Elastic scale-up (`overshoot` easing) of the isometric smartphone frame. 
   - Growth curve drawing itself dynamically from left to right with a glowing blue trail.
3. **The Sentimental Magic / Memories Bloom (Act II):** 
   - Soft color transition from clinical medical blue (`#4A90E2`) to pastel pink (`#FF8DA1`). 
   - Polaroid memory cards sliding down with subtle random rotations (-3deg to +4deg).
4. **The Privacy Revocation Shield (Act III):** 
   - Circular SVG countdown timer overlaying the pulsing QR code. 
   - Simulated thumb tap on the pink "Revoke Access" button shattering the QR code into pixel particles and terminating the clinician view session.
5. **The Generational Synthesis (Act IV):** 
   - Horizontal split-screen convergence merging physical keep-sake visuals with digital app UI into a unified center frame zoom-out.
